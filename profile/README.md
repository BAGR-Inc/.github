# BAGR

A producer live from her bedroom studio.
iPhone propped against the right monitor, because that's the good angle.
She starts by chopping a vocal from an old soul record, one from the crate her uncle left her.
Fans tap in while she digs through her kits for the right hi-hat, kick, and one or two percs.
Nothing's bouncing yet.

Every few minutes she stops to read the chat and shout somebody out. She's up to a couple hundred viewers now.
She's just as picky with her 808s. Skips about twenty.
"Can't go wrong with Spinz, man," she tells chat.
It hits.
Chat approves with fire emojis.
She nods and keeps building.

Then someone types: "how much for the beat?"
She doesn't know yet.
It's not the first time someone's asked.
"Man, if this shit had a Buy-Now button..." she thinks.
It doesn't.
So she defaults to the usual: "link in bio."

Nobody clicks. Stream ends. So does the sale.

## BAGR is that Buy-Now button.

A live shopping app for hip-hop creators. Go live, set a starting price, and fans bid for exclusive rights to the beat while it's still cooking.

She didn't know what to charge. Now the market tells her.

### where it's at

Early. There's a working MVP and five producers with multi-platinum and diamond records on board, each one recruited personally, one conversation at a time.
